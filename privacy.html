Privacy & Consent

How we handle your personal data.

Your Privacy Matters

Inclusive Development Solutions (IDS) Self-Help Group respects your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information — whether you are a member, a visitor, a partner, or someone who has interacted with us online or at an event.

We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Information We May Collect

  • Contact details — name, phone number, email address
  • Photographs, videos and voice recordings — taken at meetings, events and site visits
  • Written quotes or statements — from members, visitors or partners
  • Enquiry details — information you send us through our contact form
  • Website usage data — anonymous analytics about how visitors use our site

Why We Collect It

We use personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and plan visits
  • To communicate with members and partners
  • To share news and updates about the programme
  • To report to partners, donors and stakeholders (in aggregate or with consent)
  • To promote Kericho Community Ecotourism on our website and social media (only with consent)

Our Consent Policy for Photos, Video & Recordings

We treat photographs, videos and voice recordings as personal data. That means we only record and publish them with express, informed consent.

What this means in practice

  • Consent is sought in advance of every meeting or public engagement
  • Anyone who does not wish to be recorded is seated away from the camera and not recorded
  • Saying no has no effect on membership, benefits or support
  • Consent lasts for 24 months from the date of signature
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting our Communications Manager

What we promise

  • We will not sell your picture or pass it to advertisers
  • We will not use your picture in a way that embarrasses you or shows you in a bad light
  • We will not publish your phone number, ID number, home location or other personal details alongside your picture
  • We will not put your name next to your picture unless you have allowed it

Please note: once something has been shared publicly, other people may already have copied or shared it. We cannot always remove those copies — but we will remove it from pages we control.

Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for photos, videos or recordings at any time
  • Complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

How We Protect Your Data

We store personal data securely and only keep it for as long as necessary. Consent forms and photo records are kept safely and used only to prove that permission was given.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and anonymous analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This helps us improve our content.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. We do not use cookies to identify you personally.
