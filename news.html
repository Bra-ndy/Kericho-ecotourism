🏆 Kericho Community Ecotourism Unveils New Identity at Award Ceremony

On Sunday 13 September 2026, Inclusive Development Solutions Self-Help Group hosted the IDS Ecotourism Logo Award Ceremony at Kipchimchim — marking the end of a county-wide design competition that drew 28 entries from residents across Kericho.

The winning design — a single tea leaf with a gold trail running through its centre — was selected for its simplicity, its cultural meaning, and its suitability for reproduction on signage, certificates and uniforms. The logo was designed to express place and journey through one symbol: the footpaths that communities in Kericho have walked for generations.

The winning entry (Entrant name and details to be inserted once consent is confirmed) received a Certificate of Merit and public recognition at the ceremony.

Guests and partners in attendance

  • Hon. Eric Bett — Guest of Honour
  • Hon. Benjamin Langat, MP for Ainamoi — Invited; sent a contribution toward youth activities
  • Representatives from Ernestea Tea Company and Browns Plantation
  • Representatives from Emmanuel AGC Church
  • Officials from the County Government of Kericho
  • Village heads from Brooke
  • All 28 competition entrants and their guests

The ceremony included lunch, prize presentations, the cutting of a cake bearing the winning design, and speeches from IDS members. Sheila Chepkemoi introduced IDS and the ecotourism programme; Roy Kipngeno served as Master of Ceremonies; and Joan Cherotich gave the vote of thanks.

What's next: The new identity will be rolled out across the programme's website, signposts, certificates and merchandise — including branded t-shirts, cups and notebooks. The IDS organisation retains its own separate logo and identity.

More from 2026

14 August 2026 — Logo Competition Closed

Entries for the Kericho Community Ecotourism design competition closed on 14 August 2026. We received 28 submissions — logos, taglines and colour palettes — from residents across the county. Thank you to everyone who took part.

Late August 2026 — Shortlisting and Judging

Every entry was reviewed by our team, with shortlisting and judging running through late August. Winners were selected based on relevance, originality and suitability for the programme.
