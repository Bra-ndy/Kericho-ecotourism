Charges & Pricing

Every visit is different. So is every quote.

How Pricing Works

We don't publish fixed prices, because no two visits are the same.

The cost of your experience depends on:

  • The route you choose — some sites are closer together than others
  • How long you want to spend — half-day, full-day or multi-day
  • The size of your group — larger groups often reduce the cost per person
  • What you'd like included — transport, meals, guides, community donations
  • The season — some experiences are only available at certain times of year

We keep our pricing fair to visitors and fair to the communities who host you. When you contact us, we'll give you a clear, itemised quote — no surprises.

What's Included

Depending on your route, your quote may include:

✅ Local guide fees
✅ Site entry arrangements
✅ Community visit contributions
✅ Meals (on request)
✅ Transport coordination (on request)
✅ Certificates or mementos (on request)

Not included unless stated

❌ Personal travel insurance
❌ Transport to Kericho
❌ Personal shopping and souvenirs
❌ Tips for guides (optional but appreciated)

Where Your Money Goes

When you book with Kericho Community Ecotourism, a significant portion of what you pay goes directly to:

  • Local guides and hosts
  • Site owners and community groups
  • Conservation and maintenance of sites
  • The continued development of the programme

We are a community self-help group, not a commercial tour operator. Our goal is to make sure tourism benefits the people whose land and stories you are visiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't you publish prices?

Because every visit is different. We'd rather give you an accurate quote than a misleading average.

How quickly will I hear back?

We aim to respond within 2 working days.

Do you offer discounts for groups or schools?

Yes — larger groups often pay less per person. Tell us about your group and we'll work it out.

How do I pay?

We'll share payment options with your quote. Bank transfer and mobile money (M-Pesa) are both accepted.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes — please let us know as early as possible so we can adjust arrangements with our guides and communities.
